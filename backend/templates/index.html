The Challenge

Indian driving environments are exceptionally diverse, characterized by chaotic traffic patterns, dynamic road types, and unique hazards that conventional safety systems often fail to address.

We've developed an intelligent computer vision system specifically designed to handle these complexities, ensuring driver safety through continuous environmental monitoring and instant alerts.
